Reading this chart: there is no severity at which rules alone wins — the model and hybrid systems lead at every point, including clean text (severity 0.0), because the deterministic pass structurally cannot recall semantic fields (~0.26 recall ceiling even unstressed). The finding that does hold as predicted: rules precision decays sharply under corruption (0.990 → 0.537, not shown directly on this F1 chart — see the table below), confirming that under OCR noise, typos, and missing labels, rules don't just go quiet, they start being wrong. Hybrid's margin over rules narrows from +0.42 F1 at severity 0.0 to +0.28 at severity 1.0 as all three systems degrade together.
